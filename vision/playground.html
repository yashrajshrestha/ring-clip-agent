LookTwice

OpenCV 5 finds what happened in a doorbell clip, Strands Decider 2B decides what to do about each event, and Amazon Bedrock takes a second look when the decider is unsure.

Try it: pick a clip, click an event, and see what OpenCV found and what the agent decided. "Re-run" sends the clip through OpenCV again, live; "Ask the decider again" and "Ask the vision model" also run live.
